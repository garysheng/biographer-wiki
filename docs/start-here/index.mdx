# Biographer

*Truths about agentic biography collection: the ontology and context that make a life story capturable.*

---

A reference wiki on collecting a person's life story with AI agents. The mechanism of collection matters less than the ontology: transcripts, characters, moments, a timeline that grows, and the forms a biography projects into. Context collection is the craft. Not overengineering the ontology is the discipline.

## How this wiki is organized

- [Concepts](/concepts): the lexicon. Coined terms defined once, cross-linked throughout.
- [Principles](/principles): the discipline. What holds true across every collection effort.
- [Ontology](/ontology): the shapes. Transcripts, characters, moments, the timeline, and the projections.
- [Reference](/reference): tools, glossary, and the voice rules this wiki is written under.

The canon is being seeded now. Pages land section by section.
